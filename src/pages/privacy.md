Boorsok Club is a home recipe website run from **Kyrgyzstan**. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

## What we collect today

Boorsok Club is a simple website. Right now it **does not use cookies, analytics, ads or tracking scripts**, and our fonts are served from our own site rather than a third party.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure and running.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we don't share it.

## Links to other sites

"Save to Pinterest" and other links take you to third-party sites with their own privacy policies.

## Changes

If we add analytics, advertising, a newsletter or a shop, we'll update this page first. We'll follow the privacy laws that apply to us and to our visitors, including asking visitors from the EU and UK for consent before setting any cookies that need it.
