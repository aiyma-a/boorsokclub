**Boorsok Club** is a home kitchen for Central Asian food: the plov, manti, lagman and boorsok of Kyrgyzstan and its neighbours, written so anyone can cook them.

Every recipe here is tested at home, photographed by us, and comes with the story of where it sits on the table.
